# Holiday and Special Occasion Schedule Library

Firm precedence rule: Holiday schedule > vacation/break schedule > regular schedule. Fit the model to the family's ages, school calendar, and conflict level. For additional holiday clause options (even/odd alternation, fixed allocation, split-day models, religious holidays), see master-clause-library.md Section XI.

Holiday and Special Occasion Schedule
The parents agree to the following holiday and special occasion time-sharing schedule, which
shall override the regular weekly schedule:
Thanksgiving
● Thanksgiving Day and the long weekend (Wednesday after school/daycare through
Sunday at 6:00 PM) will alternate annually between the parents.
● The parent who has Thanksgiving in a given year will receive the second half of the
winter break (see Christmas schedule below).
Christmas/Winter Break
● The winter break will be divided into two time periods:
○ Period 1: A 5 day period that can be flexible up to 2 days before December 25
at 9:00 AM and ends at latest on December 29 at 6:00 PM
○ Period 2: A 5 day period that can be flexible up to 2 days before December 29
at 12:00 PM and ends at latest January 2 at 6:00 PM
● Parents will alternate these two time periods annually.
● The parent who has Thanksgiving in a given year shall receive Period 2 of the
Christmas break.
● The parent who does not have the child on Christmas Day is welcome to spend up
to two hours with the child on Christmas Eve or Christmas Day, if convenient for both
parties and the child.
● Parents reserve the right to adjust and revisit this annually as needed, but if they cannot
agree, both parties agree to default to this schedule.
Child’s Birthday
● The child shall remain with the parent scheduled to have the child under the regular
time-sharing plan.
● The non-scheduled parent is welcome to spend time with the child on the birthday (up to
2 hours, with the time being agreed upon by both parties), to allow both parents to
celebrate.
Parents’ Birthdays
● The child shall spend the day with each parent on their respective birthdays, regardless
of the regular schedule, provided it does not cause a major interruption in the schedule.
● If the birthday falls on a daycare/school day, the child may spend time with the parent for
at least 2 hours in the evening (schedule to be agreed upon by parents)Mother’s Day / Father’s Day
● The child shall be with Mother on Mother’s Day and Father on Father’s Day,
regardless of the regular time-sharing schedule.
Summer Holidays (Memorial Day, Juneteenth, 4th of July, Labor Day)
Can be negotiated annually - otherwise to default to the normal time sharing calendar.
