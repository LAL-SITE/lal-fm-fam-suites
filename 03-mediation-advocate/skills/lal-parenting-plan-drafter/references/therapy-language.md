# Therapeutic Provisions (Firm-Approved Language)

Attorney-approved therapeutic provisions for incorporation into a proposed Parenting Plan or settlement agreement. Use where the facts suggest therapy, conflict, emotional harm, reunification, or ongoing treatment issues. Scale from the routine clause (therapy permitted / jointly selected) in master-clause-library.md Section IV/XVIII up to this full structure. Where staff does not know how strong the therapy structure should be, draft the standard therapy-friendly clause and include this stronger structure as an alternate for attorney review.

## Individual Therapy for the Child

The child shall be immediately enrolled in individual therapy with a licensed mental health professional experienced in working with children and high-conflict family systems. The parents shall jointly select the therapist. If the parents are unable to agree within ten (10) days, the therapist shall be selected pursuant to mediation or further agreement of the parties.

## Family Therapy

The parties and the child shall participate in family therapy with a licensed mental health professional. The family therapist shall determine the appropriate structure of sessions, including but not limited to sessions with the child alone, with one parent and the child, with both parents, or with the parents only, as clinically appropriate.

## Therapist Authority

The family therapist shall have discretion to:

- Determine session structure and participation;
- Make therapeutic recommendations related to co-parenting, communication, and parenting practices;
- Recommend additional services, including but not limited to parenting coaching, reunification work, or individual therapy for either parent.

The parties shall comply with the reasonable therapeutic recommendations of the family therapist unless otherwise agreed or ordered by the Court.

## Participation and Conduct

The parents shall not attend therapy sessions together in person unless expressly recommended by the therapist. Either or both parents may appear via Zoom or similar platform as directed by the therapist.

The parents shall conduct themselves respectfully and shall not use therapy as a forum to litigate or rehash legal disputes.

## Consent and Communication

The parents shall execute all necessary releases authorizing:

- The child's individual therapist and the family therapist to communicate with one another for treatment planning purposes; and
- The therapists to provide limited information regarding attendance, participation, compliance, and general treatment progress (not content) to both parents and, if applicable, the Guardian ad Litem.

## Costs

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the costs of the child's individual therapy and family therapy shall be shared equally (50/50) by the parties.

## Relationship to Timesharing

The parties acknowledge that therapeutic progress is intended to support the child's emotional well-being and the long-term timesharing arrangement. Any discussion of a modified timesharing structure shall be informed by therapeutic recommendations and the child's best interests.

## Non-Interference

Neither parent shall interfere with, discourage, or undermine the child's participation in therapy or the therapeutic process, nor shall either parent disparage therapy, the therapist, or the other parent in the presence of the child.

## Scaling Guide

- **Permitted therapy (lightest):** Either parent may consent to counseling; other parent informed promptly. (Master library Sec. IV optional clauses.)
- **Jointly selected:** Parents jointly select provider; 10-day deadlock rule to mediation.
- **Mandatory / ongoing:** Immediate enrollment language above, therapist-authority clause, compliance requirement.
- **Reunification-tied (strongest):** Pair full structure above with phased/supervised contact from model-plan-tier4-supervised.md; expansion of timesharing conditioned on therapist recommendation and demonstrated compliance.
