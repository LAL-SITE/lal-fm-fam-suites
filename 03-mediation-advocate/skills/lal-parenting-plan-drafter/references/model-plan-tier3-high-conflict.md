# Model Plan - Tier 3: High Conflict (Control + Protection + Enforcement)

Use for high-conflict, unstable, evasive, or control-heavy cases. Specific language, firm procedures, strict communication structure, default rules, enforcement language. Pair with high-conflict-provisions.md.

VERSION 3 — CONFLICT (CONTROL + PROTECTION + ENFORCEMENT) 
STYLE OF CASE 
PARENTING PLAN 
I. PARENTS 
A. Mother is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
B. Father is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
II. CHILD(REN) 
A. This Parenting Plan applies to the following minor child(ren): 
Name: ____________________________________ 
Date of Birth: ______________________________ 
III. JURISDICTION 
A. Florida is the home state of the minor child(ren) pursuant to the Uniform Child Custody 
Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act. 
B. The Court retains continuing jurisdiction over the parties and the minor child(ren). 
IV. PARENTAL RESPONSIBILITY 
A. The parties shall have shared parental responsibility. 
B. The parents shall confer and attempt to reach agreement regarding major decisions. In the 
event the parents are unable to agree after good faith efforts to confer, the following parent shall 
have ultimate decision-making authority in the following areas: 
1. Education: __________________________ 
2. Medical and mental health: __________________________ 
3. Extracurricular activities: __________________________ C. Each parent shall make day-to-day decisions while the minor child(ren) are in that parent’s 
care. 
D. Neither parent shall unilaterally change the child’s school, enroll the child in private school, 
initiate or terminate mental health treatment, or make significant non-emergency medical 
decisions without written agreement of both parents or further order of the Court. 
E. Either parent may make emergency decisions affecting the health or safety of the minor 
child(ren), and the other parent shall be notified as soon as reasonably possible, but no later than 
twenty-four (24) hours after the emergency arises or is addressed, except where immediate 
notification is not possible due to the emergency circumstances. 
V. INFORMATION SHARING 
A. Both parents shall have equal access to all records, including school, medical, dental, and 
mental health records. 
B. Both parents may communicate directly with providers. 
C. Both parents shall be listed as emergency contacts. 
D. Each parent shall notify the other parent in writing through the parenting application of any 
change in address, telephone number, or email address within seventy-two (72) hours of such 
change. 
E. All school, medical, and activity-related information shall be shared within twenty-four (24) 
hours of receipt. 
F. Notice of all appointments shall be provided within twenty-four (24) hours of scheduling. 
VI. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENTS 
A. All non-emergency communication between the parents regarding the minor child(ren) shall 
occur through a parenting communication platform such as Talking Parents, Our Family Wizard, 
or another similar platform agreed upon by the parties or ordered by the Court. 
B. The parties shall not communicate by text, email, or telephone except in emergencies. 
C. Non-emergency communications shall be responded to within twenty-four (24) hours. 
D. Emergency communications shall be responded to as soon as reasonably possible. 
E. Communication shall be limited strictly to issues concerning the minor child(ren). F. The minor child(ren) shall not be used as messengers, intermediaries, or sources of 
information. 
VII. SCHEDULING FRAMEWORK 
A. The school calendar shall control all scheduling. 
B. Academic breaks shall begin at school dismissal and end at 6:00 p.m. on the day before school 
resumes. 
C. Undesignated school days shall follow the regular time-sharing schedule. 
D. Long weekends shall be exercised by the parent whose weekend is scheduled. 
E. Temporary changes shall only be made through written agreement on the parenting 
application. 
F. No verbal agreements shall modify this Parenting Plan. 
G. Temporary changes shall not constitute permanent modification. 
VIII. TIME-SHARING SCHEDULE 
A. The parties shall follow a 5/2 equal time-sharing schedule. 
B. One parent shall exercise time-sharing every Monday and Tuesday. 
C. The other parent shall exercise time-sharing every Wednesday and Thursday. 
D. The parties shall alternate Friday, Saturday, and Sunday on a week-on/week-off basis. 
E. Exchanges shall occur as follows: 
1. When school is in session, exchanges shall occur at school dismissal. 
2. When school is not in session, exchanges shall occur at 9:00 a.m. at the designated 
exchange location. 
F. Holiday and vacation schedules shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
IX. HOLIDAY SCHEDULE 
A. Holiday time-sharing shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
B. Thanksgiving Break shall alternate annually. C. Winter Break shall alternate as a full break annually. 
D. Spring Break shall alternate annually. 
E. Minor holidays and birthdays shall follow the regular schedule unless otherwise agreed in 
writing. 
X. SUMMER SCHEDULE 
A. Each parent shall be entitled to two (2) weeks of uninterrupted vacation time. 
B. Written notice shall be provided by April 15, including full travel details. 
C. Failure to timely provide notice shall result in forfeiture of priority selection. 
D. Vacation shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
XI. TRANSPORTATION AND EXCHANGES 
A. Exchanges shall occur at __________________________ (neutral public location or school). 
B. The parent beginning time-sharing shall provide transportation. 
C. The parties shall not have direct contact during exchanges when reasonably possible. 
D. The minor child(ren) shall be ready at the designated time with all necessary belongings. 
E. If a parent is more than thirty (30) minutes late without prior communication through the 
parenting application explaining the delay, the other parent may leave after waiting thirty (30) 
minutes, and the late parent shall not be entitled to make up the missed time-sharing. However, 
the time-sharing shall resume at the next scheduled exchange. 
F. Third-party transportation shall be permitted with advance written notice. 
G. No discussions of disputes shall occur at exchanges. 
XII. EDUCATION 
A. Major educational decisions shall be made as set forth in Section IV. 
B. The child shall remain in the current school absent written agreement or court order. 
C. School designation shall be __________________________. 
D. Both parents shall have full access to school records and events. E. The parent exercising school-night time-sharing shall ensure completion of homework and 
assignments. 
XIII. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENT AND CHILD 
A. The minor child(ren) shall have reasonable telephone or video communication with the non-time-sharing parent at a mutually agreed upon time, which shall generally occur between 7:00 
p.m. and 8:00 p.m. on school nights and at a reasonable time on non-school nights, taking into 
account the child's schedule, activities, and bedtime. 
B. The communication shall not be monitored, interrupted, or restricted. 
C. Missed communication shall be returned as soon as reasonably possible. 
XIV. MEDICAL, DENTAL, AND MENTAL HEALTH 
A. Major decisions shall be made as set forth in Section IV. 
B. Notice of all appointments shall be provided within twenty-four (24) hours of scheduling. 
C. Both parents shall have the right to attend appointments. 
D. Either parent may obtain necessary medical care during that parent’s time-sharing. 
E. Health insurance shall be maintained by __________________________ if reasonably 
available. 
F. Uncovered expenses shall be shared equally unless otherwise agreed. 
G. Documentation of uncovered medical expenses, including receipts and explanation of 
benefits, shall be provided to the other parent within thirty (30) days of payment. Reimbursement 
of the other parent's share shall be made within thirty (30) days of receipt of such documentation. 
XV. EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES 
A. All extracurricular activities must be agreed upon in writing. 
B. No activity shall be scheduled during the other parent’s time-sharing without written 
agreement. 
C. Any unauthorized activity shall be the sole financial responsibility of the enrolling parent. 
XVI. CHILDCARE AND RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL A. If a parent will be unavailable to personally care for the minor child(ren) during his or her 
scheduled time-sharing, that parent shall offer the other parent the opportunity to care for the 
child before using third-party childcare (right of first refusal) when: 
1. The parent will be unavailable to personally care for the child for more than four (4) 
consecutive hours; or 
2. The parent will be unavailable to personally care for the child overnight. 
B. The other parent shall be offered the opportunity to care for the child before third-party 
childcare is used. 
C. The other parent shall respond within four (4) hours of receiving notice. 
D. This provision shall not create additional time-sharing rights or modification. 
XVII. TRAVEL 
A. For out-of-state travel, at least fourteen (14) days’ written notice shall be provided. 
B. For international travel, at least thirty (30) days’ written notice and written consent shall be 
required. 
C. Travel notice shall include itinerary, lodging, and contact information. 
D. The parties shall cooperate in all passport and travel documentation. 
XVIII. RELOCATION 
A. Any relocation shall comply with section 61.13001, Florida Statutes. 
B. Any change of residence exceeding fifty (50) miles from the current residence shall require at 
least sixty (60) days' advance written notice to the other parent. 
C. Any move exceeding fifteen (15) miles that affects the Parenting Plan shall require mediation 
prior to implementation. 
XIX. DISPUTE RESOLUTION 
A. All disputes shall first be addressed through written communication on the parenting 
application. 
B. Except in cases of emergency or where domestic violence has been alleged or found, the 
parties shall attempt mediation prior to filing any motion to modify this Parenting Plan. However, nothing in this provision shall prevent a parent from seeking immediate court 
intervention when necessary to protect the child's welfare. 
C. Written notice of the issue shall be provided prior to mediation. 
XX. MODIFICATION 
A. No modification shall occur without written agreement approved by the Court or further order 
of the Court. 
B. Temporary deviations shall not modify this Parenting Plan. 
XXI. GENERAL PROVISIONS 
A. The parents shall not speak negatively about the other parent in the presence of the minor 
child(ren). 
B. The minor child(ren) shall not be questioned regarding the other parent’s household. 
C. The minor child(ren) shall not be exposed to conflict or litigation matters. 
D. Each parent shall foster a positive relationship between the minor child(ren) and the other 
parent. 
E. Failure to comply with this Parenting Plan may result in the other parent seeking enforcement 
through contempt proceedings or other appropriate legal remedies as provided by Florida law. 
Substantial, material, and unanticipated changes in circumstances may be grounds for 
modification of this Parenting Plan pursuant to Florida Statute 61.13(3). 
XXII. SIGNATURES 
The parties acknowledge that they have read and understand this Parenting Plan and agree to be 
bound by its terms.
