PARENTAL RESPONSIBILITY / DECISION-MAKING
Option 1 – Shared Parental Responsibility
The parties shall exercise shared parental responsibility for the minor child(ren). The parties shall confer
and attempt to reach agreement so that all major decisions affecting the welfare of the child(ren),
including but not limited to education, non-emergency medical care, mental health treatment, religion,
and extracurricular activities, are determined jointly.
Neither parent shall unilaterally make major decisions without first making a good faith effort to confer
with the other parent through the designated communication method. Each parent shall respond to
such requests within a reasonable time.
Option 2 – Shared with Ultimate Decision-Making Authority
The parties shall exercise shared parental responsibility. The parties shall confer in good faith regarding
all major decisions affecting the minor child(ren), including education, medical care, mental health
treatment, and extracurricular activities.

COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENTS
Option 1 – General Communication
The parties shall communicate respectfully regarding all matters affecting the minor child(ren). Each
parent shall keep the other informed of all significant issues affecting the child(ren), including school,
medical, and extracurricular matters.
Option 2 – Written Communication Required
All non-emergency communication shall occur in writing via text message or email. Each parent shall
respond within a reasonable time, not to exceed twenty-four (24) hours.
Option 3 – Parenting Application Required
All non-emergency communication shall occur through a parenting communication platform such as
Talking Parents or Our Family Wizard.
Each parent shall respond within twenty-four (24) hours unless circumstances require a sooner
response.

EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
Option 1 – Mutual Agreement Required
The parties shall mutually agree in writing on all extracurricular activities that require ongoing
commitment or significant expense.
Option 2 – Enrollment by Either Parent
Each parent may enroll the child(ren) in extracurricular activities during their time-sharing, provided
such activities do not substantially interfere with the other parent’s time-sharing.
Option 3 – Cost Threshold Control
Any extracurricular activity with a cost exceeding $________ shall require mutual written agreement.
MEDICAL AND MENTAL HEALTH CARE
Option 1 – Joint Decision-Making
The parties shall jointly make all major medical and mental health decisions affecting the minor
child(ren).
Option 2 – One Parent Coordinates
__________________________ shall be responsible for scheduling and coordinating medical, dental, and
mental health appointments. Both parents shall retain decision-making authority.
Option 3 – Routine Care Allowed
Each parent may obtain routine medical care during their time-sharing. Major medical and mental
health decisions shall require mutual agreement.
CHILDCARE / RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL
Option 1 – No Right of First Refusal
Neither parent shall be required to offer the other parent additional time prior to using third-party
childcare.
Option 2 – Applies After ThresholdIf either parent requires childcare for more than ______ consecutive hours, that parent shall first offer
the other parent the opportunity to care for the child(ren).
Option 3 – Overnight Only
The right of first refusal shall apply only when overnight childcare is required.
TRAVEL
Option 1 – No Restrictions
Each parent may travel with the child(ren) during their time-sharing.
Option 2 – Notice Requirement
The traveling parent shall provide reasonable advance notice of travel to the other parent.
Option 3 – Detailed Notice
The traveling parent shall provide written notice including travel dates, destination, lodging
information, and contact information.
RELOCATION
Option 1 – Statutory Standard
Relocation shall be governed by Florida Statute 61.13001.
Option 2 – Notice Requirement
Each parent shall provide written notice of any intended change of residence.
Option 3 – Restricted Radius
Any relocation exceeding ______ miles that materially affects the Parenting Plan shall require notice and
agreement or further court order.
DISPUTE RESOLUTION
Option 1 – Direct Communication
The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes through direct communication.
Option 2 – Mediation Required
The parties shall participate in mediation prior to seeking court intervention.
Option 3 – Tiered Resolution ProcessThe parties shall resolve disputes as follows:
1. Direct communication
2. Mediation
3. Court intervention
MODIFICATION
Option 1 – Written Agreement
Any modification to this Parenting Plan must be in writing and signed by both parties.
Option 2 – Court Approval Required
Any modification to this Parenting Plan must be approved by the Court to be enforceable.
ENFORCEMENT
Standard Provision
Failure to comply with the terms of this Parenting Plan may result in enforcement proceedings,
including contempt, modification, or other relief as permitted by law.
