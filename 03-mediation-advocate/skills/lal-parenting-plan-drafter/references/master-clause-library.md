Ideas, Thoughts, and Tips
Use full legal names and complete birth dates. If there are multiple children with different needs, this
is a good reminder point to check whether any section later needs child-specific language instead of
one-size-fits-all language. If confidentiality or domestic violence issues exist, do not casually insert a
protected address just because the form has a blank for it. That is how administrative sloppiness
turns into a security problem.

stuck in a storm, the plan should tell the parents what happens next instead of inviting a fresh legal
circus.
