# Model Plan - Tier 2: Moderate Conflict (Structured + Accountability)

Use for moderately contested cases. Tightened communication rules, notice deadlines, exchange mechanics, decision procedures.

VERSION 2 — MODERATE CONFLICT (STRUCTURED + ACCOUNTABILITY) 
STYLE OF CASE 
PARENTING PLAN 
I. PARENTS 
A. Mother is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
B. Father is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
II. CHILD(REN) 
A. This Parenting Plan applies to the following minor child(ren): 
Name: ____________________________________ 
Date of Birth: ______________________________ 
III. JURISDICTION 
A. Florida is the home state of the minor child(ren) pursuant to the Uniform Child Custody 
Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act (UCCJEA), as codified in Florida Statutes Chapter 61, Part II. 
B. The Court retains continuing jurisdiction over the parties and the minor child(ren). 
IV. PARENTAL RESPONSIBILITY 
A. The parties shall have shared parental responsibility. 
B. The parents shall confer in good faith and jointly make all major decisions affecting the minor 
child(ren), including decisions regarding education, healthcare, mental health treatment, and 
extracurricular activities. 
C. Each parent shall make day-to-day decisions while the minor child(ren) are in that parent’s 
care. 
D. Neither parent shall unilaterally change the child’s school, enroll the child in private school, 
initiate or terminate therapy, authorize elective surgery, or make significant medical decisions 
(including but not limited to decisions regarding mental health medication, non-emergency surgical procedures, or changes in primary care providers) without written agreement of the other 
parent or further order of the Court. 
E. If the parents are unable to reach agreement after good faith discussion, the parties shall 
participate in mediation prior to seeking court intervention, except in emergencies. 
F. Either parent may make emergency decisions affecting the health or safety of the minor 
child(ren) when circumstances require immediate action to prevent serious harm. The other 
parent shall be notified as soon as reasonably possible, but no later than two (2) hours after the 
emergency arises or is addressed, or immediately upon the child's stabilization if the emergency 
prevents earlier notification. 
V. INFORMATION SHARING 
A. Both parents shall have equal access to all records concerning the minor child(ren), including 
school, medical, dental, and mental health records. 
B. Both parents may communicate directly with teachers, medical providers, therapists, and other 
professionals involved in the child’s care. 
C. Both parents shall be listed as emergency contacts for all schools, daycare providers, and 
medical providers. 
D. Each parent shall notify the other parent in writing through the designated parenting 
communication platform of any change in address, telephone number, or email address within 
twenty-four (24) hours of such change. 
E. Each parent shall provide the other parent with copies of all school notices, report cards, 
disciplinary notices, and significant communications within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt. 
F. Each parent shall provide notice of any scheduled medical, dental, or therapy appointment at 
least forty-eight (48) hours in advance, unless the appointment is urgent. 
G. Each parent shall provide prompt notice of any illness, injury, or significant medical issue 
affecting the minor child(ren). 
VI. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENTS 
A. All non-emergency communication regarding the minor child(ren) shall occur through a 
parenting communication platform. The parties shall mutually select and equally share the cost 
of such platform within thirty (30) days of the entry of this Parenting Plan. Acceptable platforms 
include, but are not limited to, Talking Parents, Our Family Wizard, or AppClose. B. Text messages and phone calls shall be used only for emergencies or time-sensitive issues 
requiring immediate attention. 
C. Non-emergency communications shall be responded to within twenty-four (24) hours. 
D. Emergency communications concerning the health or safety of the minor child(ren) shall be 
responded to as soon as reasonably possible, but no later than two (2) hours, unless 
circumstances prevent such response, in which case response shall be made at the earliest 
opportunity. 
E. All communications shall be respectful and limited to issues concerning the minor child(ren). 
F. The parties shall not use the minor child(ren) as messengers or intermediaries. 
VII. SCHEDULING FRAMEWORK 
A. The school calendar shall control the time-sharing schedule. 
B. Academic breaks shall begin at the time of school dismissal on the last day of school before 
the break and end at 6:00 p.m. on the day before school resumes, unless otherwise stated in this 
Parenting Plan. 
C. Undesignated school days, including teacher workdays, shall follow the regular time-sharing 
schedule. 
D. If a school holiday is attached to a weekend, the weekend shall be extended through the 
holiday period. 
E. Temporary changes to the schedule shall only be made by written agreement through the 
parenting communication platform. 
F. A parent requesting a temporary change shall provide at least three (3) days’ advance written 
notice through the parenting communication platform, except in cases of documented emergency 
or illness. 
G. Temporary changes shall not constitute a permanent modification of this Parenting Plan. 
VIII. TIME-SHARING SCHEDULE 
A. The parties shall follow a 5/2/2/5 rotating time-sharing schedule as detailed below. 
B. Mother/Father [designate which parent] shall exercise time-sharing every Monday and 
Tuesday. C. Mother/Father [designate which parent] shall exercise time-sharing every Wednesday and 
Thursday. 
D. The parties shall alternate Friday, Saturday, and Sunday on a week-on/week-off basis, with 
Mother/Father [designate which parent] exercising the first weekend beginning [insert specific 
date]. 
E. Exchanges shall occur as follows: 
1. When school is in session, exchanges shall occur at school dismissal. 
2. When school is not in session, exchanges shall occur at 9:00 a.m. at the residence of the 
parent ending time-sharing, unless the parties agree in writing to an alternate location. 
F. Holiday and vacation schedules shall take precedence over the regular time-sharing schedule. 
IX. HOLIDAY SCHEDULE 
A. Holiday time-sharing shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
B. Thanksgiving Break shall be exercised by Mother in even-numbered years and Father in odd-numbered years, beginning at the time of school dismissal on the last day of school before the 
break and ending at 6:00 p.m. on the day before school resumes. 
C. Winter Break shall alternate annually, with Mother exercising the entire break in even-numbered years and Father exercising the entire break in odd-numbered years, beginning at the 
time of school dismissal on the last day of school before the break and ending at 6:00 p.m. on the 
day before school resumes. 
D. Spring Break shall alternate annually, with Mother exercising the break in even-numbered 
years and Father in odd-numbered years. 
E. Minor holidays shall follow the regular time-sharing schedule. 
F. The child(ren)’s birthdays and each parent’s birthday shall follow the regular time-sharing 
schedule. 
X. SUMMER SCHEDULE 
A. Each parent shall be entitled to two (2) weeks of uninterrupted vacation time. 
B. Vacation time shall take precedence over the regular schedule. C. Written notice of vacation dates, travel destination, lodging address, and 24-hour contact 
information shall be provided through the parenting communication platform no later than April 
15 of each year. 
D. In even-numbered years, Mother shall select vacation time first; in odd-numbered years, 
Father shall select first. 
E. In the event of a scheduling conflict, the parent with priority selection shall control. 
F. Absent designated vacation periods, the regular time-sharing schedule shall continue. 
XI. TRANSPORTATION AND EXCHANGES 
A. The parent beginning time-sharing shall provide transportation. 
B. When school is in session, exchanges shall occur at school. 
C. When school is not in session, exchanges shall occur at the residence of the parent ending 
time-sharing. 
D. Each parent shall have the child(ren) ready at the scheduled exchange time with all necessary 
clothing, school materials, medication, and personal belongings. 
E. If a parent is more than thirty (30) minutes late to an exchange without prior communication 
through the parenting application explaining the delay, the waiting parent may leave after thirty 
(30) minutes and that time-sharing period shall be deemed forfeited unless the parties otherwise 
agree in writing. The parent who forfeits time-sharing shall not be entitled to make-up time. 
F. The parties shall not engage in discussion of disputes during exchanges. 
G. Either parent may designate a responsible adult age 21 or older with a valid driver's license 
and automobile insurance for transportation with at least 24 hours' prior written notice through 
the parenting communication platform, provided such person is not a paramour or romantic 
partner unless the parties agree otherwise in writing. 
XII. EDUCATION 
A. The parents shall jointly make all major educational decisions. 
B. The minor child(ren) shall remain in their current school unless otherwise agreed in writing or 
ordered by the Court. 
C. The child(ren) shall be designated as residing with __________________________ 
[Mother/Father] for school enrollment and registration purposes only. This designation does not 
affect the equal time-sharing arrangement or either parent's rights under this Parenting Plan. D. Both parents shall have full access to school records and may attend all school events, 
meetings, and conferences. 
E. The parent exercising time-sharing on school nights shall ensure that the child(ren) complete 
all homework and school assignments to the best of the child's ability and shall communicate any 
difficulties or concerns to the other parent and the teacher as appropriate. 
XIII. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENT AND CHILD 
A. The minor child(ren) shall have reasonable telephone, video, or electronic communication 
with the parent who is not exercising time-sharing. 
B. Each parent shall encourage and facilitate such communication. 
C. Neither parent shall monitor, interfere with, or restrict such communication absent a 
legitimate concern for the child’s welfare. 
XIV. MEDICAL, DENTAL, AND MENTAL HEALTH 
A. The parents shall jointly make all major non-emergency medical, dental, and mental health 
decisions. 
B. Notice of all non-emergency appointments shall be provided at least forty-eight (48) hours in 
advance. 
C. Both parents shall have the right to attend appointments. 
D. Either parent may obtain necessary medical care during that parent’s time-sharing. 
E. The other parent shall be notified promptly of any illness, injury, or treatment. 
F. Health insurance shall be maintained by __________________________ if reasonably 
available. 
G. Uncovered medical, dental, and mental health expenses shall be shared equally unless 
otherwise agreed. 
H. Requests for reimbursement shall be submitted with documentation within twenty (20) days, 
and payment shall be made within twenty (20) days thereafter. 
XV. EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES 
A. The parents shall mutually agree on all extracurricular activities. B. No activity shall be scheduled during the other parent’s time-sharing without that parent’s 
written agreement. 
C. If a parent enrolls the child(ren) in an activity without agreement, that parent shall be solely 
responsible for all associated costs. 
D. Both parents shall have the right to attend activities and events. 
XVI. CHILDCARE AND RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL 
A. If a parent requires childcare for a period exceeding eight (8) consecutive hours or overnight 
during that parent’s time-sharing, that parent shall first offer the other parent the opportunity to 
care for the child. 
B. The other parent shall respond within a reasonable time, not to exceed four (4) hours. 
C. Exercise or waiver of this provision shall not create a modification of the Parenting Plan. 
XVII. TRAVEL 
A. Each parent may travel with the minor child(ren) during that parent’s time-sharing. 
B. For out-of-state travel, at least seven (7) days’ written notice shall be provided, including 
destination, dates, lodging, and contact information. 
C. The parties shall cooperate in executing all necessary travel documents, including passports. 
XVIII. RELOCATION 
A. Any relocation shall comply with section 61.13001, Florida Statutes. 
B. Any move of more than ten (10) miles that materially affects school, transportation, or 
exchanges shall require advance written notice and good-faith discussion. 
C. Any move of more than fifteen (15) miles that materially affects the Parenting Plan shall 
require mediation prior to implementation, absent emergency circumstances. 
XIX. DISPUTE RESOLUTION 
A. The parties shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith. 
B. Except in emergencies, the parties shall participate in mediation prior to filing any court 
action. C. Written notice of the dispute shall be provided prior to initiating legal proceedings. 
XX. MODIFICATION 
A. This Parenting Plan may be modified only by written agreement approved by the Court or 
further order of the Court. 
B. Temporary deviations shall not constitute permanent modification. 
XXI. GENERAL PROVISIONS 
A. The parents shall act in the best interests of the minor child(ren). 
B. Each parent shall foster a positive relationship between the minor child(ren) and the other 
parent. 
C. The minor child(ren) shall not be exposed to parental conflict or litigation issues. 
XXII. SIGNATURES 
The parties acknowledge that they have read and understand this Parenting Plan and agree to be 
bound by its terms.
