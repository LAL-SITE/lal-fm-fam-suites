# Model Plan - Tier 1: Low Conflict (Structured & Practical)

Use for functional/agreed cases. Cleaner structure, standard provisions, reasonable flexibility language permitted.

VERSION 1 — LOW CONFLICT (STRUCTURED & PRACTICAL) 
STYLE OF CASE 
PARENTING PLAN 
I. PARENTS 
A. Mother is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
B. Father is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
II. CHILD(REN) 
A. This Parenting Plan applies to the following minor child(ren): 
Name: ____________________________________ 
Date of Birth: ______________________________ 
III. JURISDICTION 
A. Florida is the home state of the minor child(ren) pursuant to the Uniform Child Custody 
Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act. 
B. The Court retains continuing jurisdiction over the parties and the minor child(ren). 
IV. PARENTAL RESPONSIBILITY 
A. The parties shall have shared parental responsibility. 
B. The parents shall confer and jointly make all major decisions affecting the minor child(ren), 
including education, healthcare, and other significant matters. C. Each parent shall make day-to-day decisions while the minor child(ren) are in that parent’s 
care. 
D. Neither parent shall unilaterally change the child’s school, enroll the child in private school, 
or otherwise alter educational placement without written agreement or court order. 
E. Either parent may make emergency decisions affecting the health or safety of the minor 
child(ren), and the other parent shall be notified as soon as reasonably possible. 
V. INFORMATION SHARING 
A. Both parents shall have equal access to all records, including school, medical, dental, and 
related records. 
B. Both parents may communicate directly with teachers, providers, and professionals. 
C. Both parents shall be listed as emergency contacts. 
D. Each parent shall notify the other of any change in address, phone number, or email within 
twenty-four (24) hours.ten (10) days.
E. Each parent shall promptly share important information regarding the minor child(ren). 
VI. SCHEDULING FRAMEWORK 
A. The school calendar shall control the schedule. 
B. Academic breaks shall begin at school dismissal and end at 6:00 p.m. on the day before school 
resumes, unless otherwise agreed. 
C. Undesignated school days shall follow the regular time-sharing schedule. 
D. If a school holiday is attached to a weekend, the weekend shall be extended to include that 
day. 
E. Temporary changes may be made by written agreement of the parties. 
F. A parent requesting a temporary change shall provide reasonable advance notice, which shall 
generally be at least three (3) days except in emergencies or when circumstances make such 
notice impractical. 
G. Temporary changes shall not constitute a permanent modification. VII. TIME-SHARING SCHEDULE 
A. The parties shall follow a 5/2 equal time-sharing schedule. 
B. One parent shall exercise time-sharing every Monday and Tuesday. 
C. The other parent shall exercise time-sharing every Wednesday and Thursday. 
D. The parties shall alternate Friday, Saturday, and Sunday on a week-on/week-off basis. 
E. Exchanges shall occur as follows: 
1. When school is in session, exchanges shall occur at school dismissal. 
2. When school is not in session, exchanges shall occur at 9:00 a.m. unless otherwise agreed 
in writing. 
F. Holiday and vacation schedules shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
VIII. HOLIDAY SCHEDULE 
A. Holiday time-sharing shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
B. Thanksgiving Break shall be exercised by Mother in even-numbered years and Father in odd-numbered years, beginning at school dismissal and ending at 6:00 p.m. the day before school 
resumes. 
C. Winter Break shall be divided equally between the parents, with the first half exercised by 
Mother in even-numbered years and Father in odd-numbered years, and the second half 
exercised by the other parent. The break shall be divided at 2:00 p.m. on the day that falls at the 
midpoint of the school's winter break. 
D. Spring Break shall be exercised by Mother in even-numbered years and Father in odd-numbered years. 
E. Minor holidays shall follow the regular schedule. 
F. The child(ren)’s birthdays and each parent’s birthday shall follow the regular schedule. 
IX. SUMMER SCHEDULE A. Each parent shall be entitled to two (2) weeks of uninterrupted vacation time. 
B. Vacation time shall take precedence over the regular schedule. 
C. Written notice of vacation dates, travel location, and contact information shall be provided no 
later than sixty (60) days prior to the intended vacation or April 15 each year, whichever occurs 
first. 
D. In even-numbered years, Mother shall select vacation first. In odd-numbered years, Father 
shall select first. 
E. Absent vacation time, the regular schedule shall continue. 
X. TRANSPORTATION AND EXCHANGES 
A. The parent beginning time-sharing shall provide transportation. 
B. When school is in session, exchanges shall occur at school. 
C. When school is not in session, exchanges shall occur at the residence of the parent ending 
time-sharing. 
D. Each parent shall have the child(ren) ready on time with necessary clothing, school items, and 
personal belongings. 
E. If a parent is more than thirty (30) minutes late without reasonable communication or 
justification, the other parent may proceed with alternative arrangements, but the late parent shall 
retain a make-up period to be exercised within seven (7) days by mutual agreement. 
F. Either parent may designate a responsible adult for transportation with reasonable notice. 
XI. EDUCATION 
A. The parents shall jointly make major educational decisions. 
B. The minor child(ren) shall remain in their current school unless otherwise agreed or ordered. 
C. The child(ren) shall be designated as residing with ________________________ for school 
purposes. 
D. Both parents may access school records and attend school events. E. The parent exercising school-night time-sharing shall ensure homework completion. 
XII. COMMUNICATION 
A. The parents shall communicate directly regarding the child(ren). 
B. Non-emergency communications regarding the child(ren) shall be responded to within a 
reasonable time, not to exceed forty-eight (48) hours, excluding weekends and holidays. 
C. The parents shall communicate respectfully. 
D. The parents shall not use the child(ren) as messengers. 
E. The child(ren) shall have reasonable telephone, video, and electronic communication with the 
non-time-sharing parent. Such communication shall generally occur at a mutually agreed time 
that does not interfere with the child(ren)'s activities, homework, or bedtime. Neither parent shall 
monitor, record, or interfere with the child(ren)'s communications with the other parent. 
XIII. MEDICAL AND HEALTHCARE 
A. The parents shall jointly make major non-emergency medical decisions. 
B. Either parent may obtain necessary routine and emergency medical care during that parent’s 
time-sharing. Routine care includes annual check-ups, sick visits, and treatment of minor injuries 
or illnesses. Emergency care includes any treatment necessary to address an immediate threat to 
the child's health or safety. 
C. The other parent shall be notified promptly of medical issues and appointments. 
D. Health insurance shall be maintained by ______________________ if reasonably available. 
E. Uncovered medical, dental, optical, and prescription expenses exceeding $250 per child per 
year shall be shared equally by the parents. Each parent shall be solely responsible for the first 
$250 per child per year in such expenses incurred during that parent's time-sharing. For expenses 
exceeding this threshold, the parent incurring the expense shall provide documentation to the 
other parent within thirty (30) days. 
F. Reimbursement shall be made within thirty (30) days of receipt of documentation, which shall 
include itemized bills, explanation of benefits, and proof of payment. XIV. EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES 
A. The parents shall mutually agree on extracurricular activities. 
B. If one parent enrolls the child in an extracurricular activity without the other parent's written 
agreement, that parent shall be solely responsible for all costs associated with the activity. 
However, the enrolling parent shall provide the other parent with reasonable notice of the 
activity schedule, and both parents may attend the child's participation in the activity. 
C. Both parents may attend activities and events. 
XV. CHILDCARE AND RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL 
A. Each parent may arrange childcare during that parent’s time-sharing. 
B. If a parent requires childcare for more than eight (8) consecutive hours, that parent shall first 
offer the other parent the opportunity to care for the child. The other parent shall respond within 
two (2) hours of receiving the request. If the other parent does not respond within this timeframe 
or declines, the requesting parent may arrange alternative childcare. 
XVI. TRAVEL 
A. Each parent may travel with the child during that parent’s time-sharing. 
B. At least seven (7) days' advance written notice shall be provided for out-of-state travel, 
including destination, lodging address, contact information, and travel dates. For international 
travel, at least thirty (30) days' advance written notice shall be provided. 
C. The parents shall cooperate in executing documents necessary for travel, including passport 
applications and renewals. Neither parent shall retain possession of the child(ren)'s passport(s) 
except during that parent's time-sharing or planned international travel. At all other times, 
passports shall be maintained in a mutually agreed secure location or alternately by each parent. 
XVII. RELOCATION 
A. Any relocation as defined in section 61.13001, Florida Statutes, shall comply with all 
requirements of that statute, including but not limited to the notice, petition, and response 
requirements set forth therein. B. Any move exceeding ten (10) miles from the current residence that materially affects school 
attendance or the time-sharing schedule shall require at least sixty (60) days' advance written 
notice and good-faith discussion between the parents. 
C. Any move exceeding fifteen (15) miles that materially affects the schedule may require 
mediation or modification. 
XVIII. DISPUTE RESOLUTION 
A. The parents shall attempt to resolve disputes in good faith. 
B. Except in cases involving emergencies, domestic violence, child abuse, or other circumstances 
making mediation inappropriate, the parties shall attend mediation and make a good faith effort 
to resolve disputes prior to filing any motion to modify or enforce this Parenting Plan. Each party 
shall be responsible for their own mediation costs unless otherwise agreed or ordered. 
XIX. MODIFICATION 
A. This Parenting Plan may be modified by written agreement approved by the Court or further 
order of the Court. 
B. Temporary deviations shall not constitute permanent modification. 
XX. GENERAL PROVISIONS 
A. The parents shall act in the best interests of the child(ren). 
B. Each parent shall encourage a positive relationship between the child(ren) and the other 
parent. 
C. The child(ren) shall not be exposed to parental conflict. 
XXI. SIGNATURES 
The parties acknowledge that they have read and understand this Parenting Plan and agree to be 
bound by its terms.
