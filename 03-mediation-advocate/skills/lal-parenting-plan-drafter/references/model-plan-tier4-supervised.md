# Model Plan - Tier 4: High Risk / Supervised / Therapeutic (Fully Structured)

Use for safety concerns, supervised or phased contact, reunification, therapy conditions, or serious restrictions. Pair with therapy-language.md and high-conflict-provisions.md.

VERSION 4 — HIGH RISK / SUPERVISED / THERAPEUTIC (FULLY STRUCTURED) 
STYLE OF CASE 
PARENTING PLAN
I. PARENTS 
A. Mother is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
B. Father is ________________________________, whose address is 
____________________________________________, telephone number is 
______________________, and email address is ______________________________. 
II. CHILD(REN) 
A. This Parenting Plan applies to the following minor child(ren): 
Name: ____________________________________ 
Date of Birth: ______________________________ 
III. JURISDICTION 
A. Florida is the home state of the minor child(ren). 
B. The Court retains continuing jurisdiction. 
IV. PARENTAL RESPONSIBILITY 
A. The parties shall have shared parental responsibility; however, 
__________________________ shall have ultimate decision-making authority regarding 
education, medical care, mental health treatment, and therapeutic services. 
B. The other parent shall not unilaterally initiate, change, or terminate therapy, schooling, or 
medical care. 
C. Each parent shall make day-to-day decisions during their authorized contact. 
D. Emergency decisions may be made as necessary, with immediate notice to the other parent. 
V. PURPOSE AND SAFETY FRAMEWORK A. This Parenting Plan is implemented due to concerns affecting the safety, stability, and/or 
emotional well-being of the minor child(ren). 
B. All contact between the child(ren) and __________________________ shall occur in a 
structured and supervised manner as outlined below. 
C. Advancement of time-sharing shall be gradual, structured, and based on demonstrated 
compliance and professional recommendations. 
VI. SUPERVISED TIME-SHARING 
A. __________________________ shall have supervised time-sharing. 
B. Time-sharing shall occur one (1) time per week for two (2) hours unless otherwise agreed in 
writing or modified by court order. 
C. Visits shall occur at: 
____________________________________________ (supervision center or approved location). 
D. Supervision shall be conducted by: 
1. A professional supervisor; or
2. A mutually agreed third party approved in writing. 
E. The supervising individual shall: 
1. Remain present at all times;
2. Ensure the safety of the child(ren);
3. Have authority to terminate the visit if concerns arise. 
F. The supervised parent shall not leave the supervision setting with the child(ren). 
G. The supervised parent shall comply with all rules of the supervision provider. 
H. Costs of supervision shall be paid by __________________________, unless otherwise 
agreed. 
I. If the supervised parent cancels a visit with less than twenty-four (24) hours’ notice, that visit 
shall be forfeited. 
J. If the supervising provider cancels, the visit shall be rescheduled within seven (7) days if 
possible. 
VII. THERAPEUTIC REQUIREMENTS A. The minor child(ren) shall participate in therapy with a licensed therapist selected by 
__________________________. 
B. The supervised parent shall participate in: 
1. Individual therapy; and
2. Any reunification or family therapy recommended. 
C. The supervised parent shall provide proof of participation upon request. 
D. The therapist may communicate general progress and recommendations to both parents. 
E. The therapist shall not be required to provide detailed session content unless ordered by the 
Court. 
VIII. PHASED-IN TIME-SHARING STRUCTURE 
A. Time-sharing shall progress in phases as follows: 
Phase 1 — Supervised Contact
As set forth in Section VI. 
Phase 2 — Expanded Supervised Contact
May include increased frequency or duration upon written recommendation of the therapist and 
mutual agreement or court approval. 
Phase 3 — Therapeutic / Transitional Contact
May include supervised outings or extended visits based on therapist recommendation. 
Phase 4 — Unsupervised Daytime Contact
Only upon written recommendation of the therapist and court approval. 
B. Advancement between phases shall require: 
1. Consistent compliance with this Parenting Plan;
2. No safety concerns during visits;
3. Written recommendation from the therapist;
4. Agreement of the parties or court order. 
C. No phase shall automatically advance based on time alone. 
IX. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENT AND CHILD A. The child(ren) shall have two (2) scheduled video or telephone communications per week, 
each not exceeding fifteen (15) minutes. 
B. Communication shall occur at agreed times or, absent agreement, at 7:00 p.m. on designated 
days. 
C. Communication may be monitored if recommended by the therapist. 
D. The supervised parent shall not discuss litigation, the other parent, or inappropriate topics. 
X. COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENTS 
A. All communication shall occur exclusively through a parenting communication application. 
B. No direct communication shall occur except in emergencies. 
C. Non-emergency messages shall be responded to within twenty-four (24) hours. 
XI. TRANSPORTATION AND EXCHANGES 
A. The non-supervised parent shall transport the child(ren) to and from the supervision location. 
B. The parents shall have no direct contact during exchanges. 
C. Exchanges shall occur through the supervision provider or at a designated neutral location. 
XII. EDUCATION 
A. All educational decisions shall be made by __________________________. 
B. The child(ren) shall remain in their current school unless otherwise ordered. 
C. Both parents shall have access to school records. 
XIII. MEDICAL AND MENTAL HEALTH 
A. All major decisions shall be made by __________________________. 
B. The supervised parent shall have access to information but shall not make independent 
decisions. 
C. The supervised parent shall comply with all recommended treatment programs. XIV. SAFETY CONDITIONS 
A. The supervised parent shall not: 
1. Consume alcohol or illegal substances prior to or during visits;
2. Be impaired during contact;
3. Engage in conduct that endangers the child(ren). 
B. The supervised parent shall comply with all court-ordered programs, including counseling or 
treatment. 
C. Any violation may result in immediate suspension of time-sharing. 
XV. TRAVEL 
A. The supervised parent shall not remove the child(ren) from the supervision setting. 
B. No overnight or out-of-state travel shall occur without court approval. 
XVI. RELOCATION 
A. Any relocation shall comply with Florida law. 
B. Any move affecting supervision or therapy shall require court approval. 
XVII. DISPUTE RESOLUTION 
A. Due to safety concerns, either party may seek court intervention without mediation if 
necessary. 
B. Mediation may be required at the Court’s discretion. 
XVIII. MODIFICATION 
A. Modification shall require court approval. 
B. Any request to expand time-sharing shall be supported by therapist recommendation and 
demonstrated compliance. 
XIX. GENERAL PROVISIONS A. The safety and well-being of the child(ren) shall control all decisions. 
B. All provisions shall be strictly enforced. 
C. Failure to comply may result in suspension or further restriction of time-sharing. 
XX. SIGNATURES 
The parties acknowledge that they have read and understand this Parenting Plan and agree to be 
bound by its terms.
