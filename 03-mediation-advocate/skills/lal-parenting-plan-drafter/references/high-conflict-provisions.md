# High-Conflict Provision Set (Copy-Ready with Built-In Options)

Use for Tier 3 (high-conflict) and Tier 4 (restrictive) cases, and selectively in Tier 2 where accountability is needed. Includes platform-only communication, response deadlines, dispute-notice procedure, deadlock resolution, forfeiture and deemed-waiver rules, non-disparagement, and strict enforcement.

COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENTS
A. All non-emergency communication between the parties shall occur exclusively through a parenting
communication platform such as Talking Parents or Our Family Wizard.
B. The parties shall not communicate via text message, email, or telephone except in emergencies or
for time-sensitive issues requiring immediate attention.
C. Non-emergency communications shall be responded to within twenty-four (24) hours.
D. Emergency communications shall be responded to as soon as reasonably possible, but no later than
two (2) hours.
E. All communication shall be limited strictly to issues concerning the minor child(ren).
F. Communications shall be respectful in tone and content and shall not include accusations, insults,
inflammatory language, or unrelated matters.
G. Non-emergency communication shall be limited to one message per issue unless additional
communication is reasonably necessary.
H. The parties shall not send repetitive, excessive, or harassing communications. Such conduct shall be
considered a violation of this Parenting Plan.
I. All agreements, modifications, and schedule changes must be confirmed in writing through the
parenting application. Verbal agreements shall not be enforceable unless reduced to writing.
J. All communication shall remain on the designated platform to preserve a complete and accurate
record.DISPUTE NOTICE AND RESOLUTION PROCESS
A. Prior to initiating mediation, the complaining parent shall provide written notice through the
parenting application clearly outlining:
– The specific issue
– The requested resolution
– The basis under this Parenting Plan or applicable law
B. No vague or generalized complaints shall trigger dispute resolution.
C. Upon written notice, the parties shall confer in good faith for up to seventy-two (72) hours.
D. If no agreement is reached, mediation shall be scheduled within fourteen (14) days of the written
notice.
E. No parent may file a motion related to the minor child(ren) without first completing mediation,
except in defined emergencies.
F. Emergencies are limited to:
– Immediate risk of harm to the child
– Medical emergencies
– Unlawful withholding of the child
G. Failure to comply with this section may be considered in awarding attorney’s fees and sanctions.
DECISION-MAKING / DEADLOCK RESOLUTION
A. The parties shall confer in good faith regarding all major decisions affecting the minor child(ren),
including education, medical care, mental health treatment, and extracurricular activities.
B. If the parties are unable to reach agreement within seventy-two (72) hours:
Option 1: The parties shall participate in mediation prior to seeking court intervention.
Option 2: __________________________ shall have ultimate decision-making authority regarding the
disputed issue.
Option 3: The status quo shall remain in place until agreement or court order.
C. No party shall take unilateral action and later claim urgency to justify that decision.
D. Nothing in this section shall prevent either party from seeking immediate court intervention in the
event of an emergency.
EDUCATION CONTROL
A. The minor child(ren) shall remain enrolled in their current school unless otherwise agreed in writing
or ordered by the Court.B. Neither parent shall enroll the child(ren) in a different school, including private school, tutoring
program, or specialized educational program, without the written agreement of the other parent or
further order of the Court.
C. Both parents shall have equal access to all educational records and may communicate directly with
school personnel.
D. All school-related decisions, including applications, transfers, and specialized programs, require
mutual agreement unless otherwise specified.
E. The parent exercising time-sharing on school nights shall ensure that the child attends school on
time and completes all assignments.
EXTRACURRICULAR ACTIVITIES
A. The parties shall mutually agree in writing on all extracurricular activities.
B. No extracurricular activity shall be scheduled during the other parent’s time-sharing without that
parent’s written agreement.
C. If the parties agree to an activity, both parents shall ensure the child’s attendance regardless of
whose time-sharing it is.
D. If a parent enrolls the child in an activity without agreement, that parent shall be solely responsible
for all associated costs and transportation.
EXCHANGE AND TRANSPORTATION
A. The parent beginning time-sharing shall be responsible for transportation unless otherwise agreed.
B. Exchanges shall occur at:
Option 1: The child’s school when school is in session.
Option 2: A neutral public location: __________________________.
Option 3: The residence of the parent ending time-sharing.
C. The parties shall not engage in conversation during exchanges except as necessary for the child’s
immediate needs.
D. The parties shall remain in their respective vehicles during exchanges when reasonably possible.
E. No third-party interference shall occur during exchanges.
F. The child shall transition directly between parents without delay.
G. Either parent may designate a responsible adult for transportation with advance written notice.
LATE / MISSED EXCHANGESA. Each parent shall be punctual for all exchanges.
B. If a parent is more than thirty (30) minutes late without communication through the parenting
application, the exchange shall be deemed forfeited unless otherwise agreed in writing.
C. Repeated late arrivals, defined as three (3) or more occurrences within a sixty (60) day period, shall
be considered a pattern of noncompliance.
D. Missed time shall not automatically entitle a parent to make-up time absent written agreement.
E. Chronic noncompliance may be grounds for enforcement, modification, or sanctions.
RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL
A. The right of first refusal shall apply under the following circumstances:
Option 1: Any absence exceeding eight (8) consecutive hours.
Option 2: Any absence exceeding six (6) consecutive hours.
Option 3: Any overnight absence.
B. The parent requiring childcare shall notify the other parent through the parenting application.
C. The other parent shall have:
Option 1: Two (2) hours to respond.
Option 2: Four (4) hours to respond.
D. Failure to respond within the required time shall be deemed a waiver of the opportunity.
E. Exercise or waiver of this provision shall not constitute a modification of the Parenting Plan.
CHILD PROTECTION / NON-DISPARAGEMENT
A. Neither parent shall speak negatively about the other parent in the presence of the minor child(ren).
B. Neither parent shall question the child regarding the other parent’s household, activities, or personal
matters.
C. The minor child(ren) shall not be used to convey messages between the parents.
D. The minor child(ren) shall not be exposed to litigation issues or parental conflict.
OVER-COMMUNICATION / HARASSMENT CONTROL
A. Non-emergency communication shall be limited to one message per issue unless additional
communication is reasonably necessary.
B. Excessive, repetitive, or harassing communication shall be considered a violation of this Parenting
Plan.MEDICAL / THERAPY CONTROL
A. The parties shall jointly make major medical and mental health decisions unless otherwise specified.
B. Neither parent shall initiate, terminate, or alter therapy, counseling, or medical treatment without
the written agreement of the other parent or further order of the Court.
C. The child(ren) shall participate in therapy with a mutually agreed therapist or court-approved
provider.
D. Neither parent shall attempt to influence, interfere with, or undermine the therapeutic process.
E. Therapy shall not be used as a forum to litigate disputes or assign blame.
F. Both parents shall have access to general information regarding the child(ren)’s treatment, subject to
applicable confidentiality laws.
TRAVEL RESTRICTIONS
A. Each parent may travel with the child(ren) during that parent’s time-sharing subject to the following:
B. For out-of-state travel, written notice shall be provided at least:
Option 1: Seven (7) days in advance.
Option 2: Fourteen (14) days in advance.
C. Notice shall include travel dates, destination, lodging information, and contact information.
D. For international travel:
– Written consent of both parents shall be required
– Passport access shall be shared or clearly defined
– Compliance with applicable international laws shall be required
E. Failure to provide required notice may result in denial of travel or court intervention.
RELOCATION NOTICE
A. Each parent shall provide written notice of any intended change of residence.
B. Any move exceeding ten (10) miles that materially affects school, transportation, or exchanges shall
require advance notice and good-faith discussion.
C. Any move exceeding fifteen (15) miles that materially affects the Parenting Plan shall require
mediation prior to implementation or further court order.
FINANCIAL / REIMBURSEMENT
A. Requests for reimbursement shall be submitted through the parenting application with supporting
documentation within twenty (20) days of the expense.B. Failure to timely submit documentation shall waive reimbursement unless otherwise agreed.
C. Payment shall be made within twenty (20) days of receipt of documentation.
MAKE-UP TIME
A. Make-up time shall occur only upon mutual written agreement.
B. Missed time due to a parent’s failure shall not automatically entitle that parent to additional time-sharing.
GENERAL ENFORCEMENT
A. All provisions of this Parenting Plan shall be strictly enforced.
B. Failure to comply with any provision may result in enforcement proceedings, modification, attorney’s
fees, or other relief as permitted by law.
C. Repeated violations or bad-faith conduct, including repetitive or meritless filings, may be considered
in awarding attorney’s fees.
RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL
Option 1 – Eight (8) Hour Threshold
If either parent requires childcare for a period exceeding eight (8) consecutive hours during their time-sharing, that parent shall first offer the other parent the opportunity to care for the minor child(ren).
Notice shall be provided through the parenting communication application and shall include the date,
time, and duration of the requested care. The receiving parent shall have two (2) hours from the time
of notice to respond. Failure to respond within that time shall be deemed a waiver of the opportunity.
Exercise or waiver of the right of first refusal shall not constitute a modification of the Parenting Plan.
Option 2 – Six (6) Hour Threshold
If either parent requires childcare for a period exceeding six (6) consecutive hours during their time-sharing, that parent shall first offer the other parent the opportunity to care for the minor child(ren).
Notice shall be provided through the parenting communication application and shall include the date,
time, and duration of the requested care. The receiving parent shall have two (2) hours from the time
of notice to respond. Failure to respond within that time shall be deemed a waiver of the opportunity.
Exercise or waiver of the right of first refusal shall not constitute a modification of the Parenting Plan.
Option 3 – Overnight Threshold
If either parent requires overnight childcare during their time-sharing, that parent shall first offer the
other parent the opportunity to care for the minor child(ren). Notice shall be provided through the
parenting communication application and shall include the date, time, and duration of the requested
care. The receiving parent shall have four (4) hours from the time of notice to respond. Failure torespond within that time shall be deemed a waiver of the opportunity. Exercise or waiver of the right of
first refusal shall not constitute a modification of the Parenting Plan.
RESPONSE TIME OPTIONS (COMMUNICATION)
Option 1 – Standard Response Window
Non-emergency communications shall be responded to within twenty-four (24) hours of receipt
through the parenting communication application.
Option 2 – Shortened Response Window
Non-emergency communications shall be responded to within twelve (12) hours of receipt through the
parenting communication application.
Option 3 – Extended Response Window
Non-emergency communications shall be responded to within forty-eight (48) hours of receipt through
the parenting communication application.
EXCHANGE LOCATION OPTIONS
Option 1 – School-Based Exchange
All exchanges shall occur at the child(ren)’s school when school is in session. The parent ending their
time-sharing shall ensure the child(ren) are timely dropped off at school, and the parent beginning their
time-sharing shall retrieve the child(ren) from school. When school is not in session, exchanges shall
occur at a mutually agreed neutral public location.
Option 2 – Neutral Location Exchange
All exchanges shall occur at the following neutral public location: __________________________. The
parties shall arrive on time, remain in their respective vehicles when reasonably possible, and shall not
engage in unnecessary communication.
Option 3 – Residence-Based Exchange
All exchanges shall occur at the residence of the parent ending their time-sharing. The parent
beginning time-sharing shall retrieve the child(ren) at the designated time. The parties shall not enter
each other’s residence and shall limit communication to what is necessary for the child(ren).
TRAVEL NOTICE OPTIONS
Option 1 – Seven (7) Day Notice
For any out-of-state travel, the traveling parent shall provide written notice at least seven (7) days in
advance through the parenting communication application. The notice shall include travel dates,destination, lodging information, and contact information.
Option 2 – Fourteen (14) Day Notice
For any out-of-state travel, the traveling parent shall provide written notice at least fourteen (14) days
in advance through the parenting communication application. The notice shall include travel dates,
destination, lodging information, and contact information.
RESPONSE TIME FOR RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL
Option 1 – Two (2) Hour Response
The receiving parent shall have two (2) hours from the time of notice to respond to a right of first
refusal request. Failure to respond within that time shall be deemed a waiver.
Option 2 – Four (4) Hour Response
The receiving parent shall have four (4) hours from the time of notice to respond to a right of first
refusal request. Failure to respond within that time shall be deemed a waiver.
DECISION-MAKING DEADLOCK OPTIONS
Option 1 – Mandatory Mediation
If the parties are unable to reach agreement on a major decision within seventy-two (72) hours after
written discussion, the parties shall participate in mediation prior to seeking court intervention.
Option 2 – Ultimate Decision-Maker
If the parties are unable to reach agreement on a major decision within seventy-two (72) hours after
written discussion, __________________________ shall have ultimate decision-making authority regarding
the disputed issue.
Option 3 – Status Quo
If the parties are unable to reach agreement on a major decision within seventy-two (72) hours after
written discussion, the status quo shall remain in place until agreement is reached or further court
order is entered.
EXCHANGE TRANSPORTATION OPTIONS
Option 1 – Receiving Parent Transports
The parent beginning time-sharing shall be responsible for transportation of the minor child(ren).
Option 2 – Delivering Parent TransportsThe parent ending time-sharing shall be responsible for transporting the minor child(ren) to the
receiving parent.
Option 3 – Split Transportation
The parties shall equally share transportation responsibilities, with one parent responsible for pick-up
and the other responsible for return, as mutually agreed in writing.
SUMMER SELECTION PRIORITY
Option 1 – Alternating Priority by Year
If both parents request the same summer time-sharing periods, selection priority shall alternate each
year. In even-numbered years, __________________ shall have first selection priority. In odd-numbered
years, __________________ shall have first selection priority. All selections must be made in writing by a
specified deadline, and failure to timely select shall result in forfeiture of priority.
Option 2 – Fixed Priority
If both parents request the same summer time-sharing periods, __________________ shall have first
selection priority each year. The other parent shall select from the remaining available time.
Option 3 – Deadline-Based Priority
Each parent shall submit their requested summer time-sharing dates in writing through the parenting
application by __________ each year. In the event of a conflict, priority shall be determined by the
parent who submitted their request first.
RELOCATION DISTANCE OPTIONS
Option 1 – Ten (10) Mile Restriction
Any relocation exceeding ten (10) miles from the parent’s current residence that materially impacts the
Parenting Plan shall require advance written notice and good-faith discussion between the parties.
Option 2 – Fifteen (15) Mile Restriction
Any relocation exceeding fifteen (15) miles from the parent’s current residence that materially impacts
the Parenting Plan shall require mediation prior to implementation or further court order.
Option 3 – Custom Radius
Any relocation exceeding ______ miles from the parent’s current residence that materially impacts time-sharing, school, or exchanges shall require advance written notice, good-faith discussion, and
mediation prior to implementation unless otherwise agreed or ordered by the Court.
