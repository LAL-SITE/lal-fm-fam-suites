# Basic Parenting Plan Skeleton (Final Formatting Control)

This is the firm's base form. It controls the final structure, section order, and tone of every finished Parenting Plan. All drafts must map onto this skeleton, expanded or tightened per the case tier.

**III. JURISDICTION**

Florida is the home state of the minor child(ren) for purposes of the Uniform Child Custody Jurisdiction and Enforcement Act. This Parenting Plan is intended to govern the relationship between the parents as it relates to parental responsibility and time-sharing concerning the minor child(ren).

**IV. PARENTAL RESPONSIBILITY AND DECISION-MAKING**

The parties shall have shared parental responsibility for the minor child(ren). The parents shall confer and jointly make all major decisions affecting the welfare of the minor child(ren), including but not limited to decisions regarding education, healthcare, and other responsibilities unique to this family.

Each parent shall make decisions regarding the day-to-day care and control of the minor child(ren) while the minor child(ren) are with that parent.

Either parent may make emergency decisions affecting the health or safety of the minor child(ren) while the minor child(ren) are with that parent. The parent making the emergency decision shall notify the other parent as soon as reasonably possible.

**V. INFORMATION SHARING**

Unless otherwise prohibited by law, both parents shall have access to medical, dental, school, and other records concerning the minor child(ren). Both parents shall have equal rights to inspect and receive governmental agency and law enforcement records concerning the minor child(ren).

Both parents shall be listed as emergency contacts for the minor child(ren) with the child(ren)’s school, daycare, and medical providers.

Each parent shall keep the other parent informed of his or her current residential address, mailing address, telephone number, and email address. Each parent shall notify the other parent in writing within twenty-four (24) hours of any change in contact information.

**VI. SCHEDULING**

The parties shall follow the applicable school calendar in scheduling time-sharing, holidays, and school breaks.

Academic breaks shall begin at the end of the last scheduled day of classes before the break and shall end when school resumes after the break, unless otherwise specifically stated in this Parenting Plan.

The parties may make temporary changes to the schedule by mutual agreement. Any substantial or permanent change to this Parenting Plan must be made by written agreement approved by the Court or by further order of the Court.

**VII. TIME-SHARING SCHEDULE**

The parties shall exercise time-sharing with the minor child(ren) as follows:

Weekday and Weekend Schedule:

If school is in session, exchanges shall occur at school unless otherwise agreed. If school is not in session, exchanges shall occur at ______________________________________________________.

Holiday Schedule:

The holiday schedule shall take precedence over the regular time-sharing schedule.

Mother’s Day shall be with Mother every year. Father’s Day shall be with Father every year.

Thanksgiving Break shall be with Mother in even-numbered years and Father in odd-numbered years.

Winter Break shall be divided equally between the parents, with the first half in even-numbered years going to Mother and the second half to Father, and the first half in odd-numbered years going to Father and the second half to Mother.

Spring Break shall be with Mother in even-numbered years and Father in odd-numbered years.

Summer Schedule:

Unless otherwise agreed, the regular time-sharing schedule shall continue during the summer.

OR

Each parent shall be entitled to two (2) uninterrupted weeks of summer vacation time with the minor child(ren), provided that written notice is given to the other parent by ______________________ of each year.

**VIII. TRANSPORTATION AND EXCHANGE**

Unless otherwise agreed, the parent beginning time-sharing shall provide transportation for the minor child(ren).

Each parent shall have the minor child(ren) ready on time for exchange, with sufficient clothing and personal items packed and ready.

If a parent is more than thirty (30) minutes late for an exchange without contacting the other parent, the waiting parent may proceed with other plans.

Either parent may designate a responsible adult to transport or exchange the minor child(ren), provided the other parent is notified in advance.

**IX. EDUCATION**

The parents shall jointly make major educational decisions concerning the minor child(ren).

For school registration and boundary purposes, the minor child(ren) shall be designated as residing with __________________________________________.

Each parent shall have the right to obtain school records, communicate with school personnel, and attend school events, conferences, and activities.

Each parent shall ensure that the minor child(ren)’s homework, school assignments, and school materials are completed and returned during that parent’s time-sharing as appropriate.

**X. COMMUNICATION**

All communications regarding the minor child(ren) shall be between the parents. The parents shall not use the minor child(ren) as messengers to convey information, ask questions, or communicate schedule changes.

The minor child(ren) shall have reasonable telephone, text, video, or other electronic communication with the parent who is not exercising time-sharing. Such communication shall not be monitored or interrupted by the other parent.

The parents shall communicate respectfully with one another regarding the minor child(ren).

**XI. MEDICAL, DENTAL, AND HEALTHCARE ISSUES**

The parents shall jointly make major non-emergency medical, dental, and healthcare decisions for the minor child(ren).

The parent exercising time-sharing at the time of a routine or emergency medical issue may obtain necessary care for the minor child(ren), but shall notify the other parent as soon as reasonably possible.

Except in emergencies, the parties shall attempt mediation before filing a court action to enforce or modify this Parenting Plan.
