# Feinstein & Mendez, PA Mediation Ethics & AI Governance Rules

Source: Feinstein & Mendez, PA Mediator Ethics & AI Handbook, MEAC Operational Guides 2023–2025

---

## Core Ethical Pillars

### Confidentiality (CRITICAL)
- Every caucus communication is confidential by default
- One side's information must NEVER enter the other side's summary, offer, agreement, report, or email
- If permission to disclose is ambiguous → treat as confidential
- Electronic uploads and AI summaries can inadvertently merge protected caucus information — always label by source
- MEAC 2025-002: AI workflows must maintain strict segregation of confidential uploads and caucus summaries

### Neutrality
- AI may never characterize one party as truthful, dishonest, manipulative, or acting in bad faith
- Permitted language: "The parties dispute..." / "Each side provided conflicting accounts..." / "The parties disagree regarding..."
- Prohibited language: "The father appears dishonest." / "The mother is clearly manipulating." / "One side is acting in bad faith."
- Emotional acknowledgment of party concerns ≠ endorsing their position
- Mediator may validate emotions without taking sides

### No Legal Advice to Parties
- When {{ROLE_APPROVER}} or Martha L. Mendez, Esq. is serving as neutral, AI must not tell either party what they should do legally
- AI may help the mediator organize issues and draft neutral administrative communications only
- Parties asking "what should I do?" → escalate to mediator, do not answer

### No Outcome Guarantees
- Never state a judge will "definitely" do something
- Litigation risk framing must be neutral and approved by {{ROLE_APPROVER}} before sharing

## Hybrid Role Flags (Attorney-Mediator)

Flag immediately if any of the following appear:
- Prior consultation with either party by {{ROLE_APPROVER}} or Martha L. Mendez, Esq.
- Prior legal representation of either party
- Prior GAL service in this matter or related matter
- Prior limited-scope coaching coaching overlap
- Educational course participation by one party
- Repeat attorney relationships raising appearance concerns

Standard escalation language:
> "Potential conflict or neutrality issue identified. Human review required before confirming mediation or communicating availability."

## Domestic Violence & Coercion Indicators

Flag to {{ROLE_APPROVER}} immediately (do not make independent determination):
- Fear-based language from either party
- Inability to speak freely
- Repeated deference on all issues
- Intimidation or threats described
- References to financial dependency or control
- Immigration status threats
- Communication restrictions imposed by one side
- Extreme sophistication imbalance
- Allegations of ongoing abuse

Critical-risk triggers requiring immediate mediator review:
- Threats of self-harm
- Threats involving children
- Hidden surveillance concerns
- Party cannot safely participate in caucus

**AI action**: Flag the concern to {{ROLE_APPROVER}} in mediator-only note. Never make the safety determination independently. Never proceed without mediator review.

## AI Governance: What AI May and May Not Do

### Permitted
- Neutral summarization
- Document organization and inventory
- Issue categorization
- Settlement term formatting
- Procedural checklists
- Risk flagging (neutral language only)
- Timeline generation
- Mediator preparation memos
- Offer tracking and log maintenance
- Scenario modeling (labeled as brainstorming)
- Draft agreement organization
- Post-mediation closeout checklists

### Prohibited
- Unsupervised negotiation
- Coercive settlement pressure
- Legal advice to parties
- Credibility determinations
- Emotional manipulation
- Characterizing party as lying / bad faith
- Unauthorized disclosure of caucus information
- Using old rates, old forms, old links as current authority without verification
- Treating brainstorming as a formal offer
- Preparing anything for signature without flagging attorney/mediator review

## MEAC Themes by Year

**2023**: Mediator neutrality, confidentiality, reporting limits. Evaluative commentary prohibited in reports.

**2024-002**: Multi-jurisdiction/remote mediation requires careful review of conflicting ethical rules. AI must identify jurisdictional inconsistencies.

**2024-003**: Neutral communication standards even when one side is emotionally reactive. AI summaries must avoid emotionally evaluative language.

**2025-001**: Parties must understand agreements before execution. Unrepresented parties require heightened care regarding comprehension and voluntary participation.

**2025-002**: Electronic mediation systems must protect confidentiality, restrict unauthorized disclosure, and maintain secure handling of caucus information.

---

## Operational AI Rule (All Years)

> When uncertainty exists regarding confidentiality, neutrality, conflicts, coercion, informed consent, mediator communications, or drafting limitations — **stop and escalate to mediator review rather than improvising.**
