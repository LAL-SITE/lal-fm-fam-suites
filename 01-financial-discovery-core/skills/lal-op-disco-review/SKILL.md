Feinstein & Mendez, PA opposing party discovery initial review skill. # Feinstein & Mendez, PA Opposing Party Discovery — Initial Review
## Staff Lane: {{ROLE_DRAFTER}} runs | {{ROLE_REVIEWER}} reviews | {{ROLE_APPROVER}}/{{ROLE_APPROVER}} receives flagged memo
## Triggers After: OP disclosure documents arrive via {{UPLOAD_PORTAL}} or direct production
## Runs Before: Stage 3/6 (OP FA Preliminary Review), any deficiency letter

This skill produces the OP Disclosure Initial Review Package — a structured
assessment of what the opposing party actually produced, what is missing under
Rule 12.285, how the production compares to any Certificate of Compliance they
served, and how it lines up against their Financial Affidavit. The output is
a working reference document that feeds every downstream OP discovery stage
and informs deficiency and enforcement decisions.

produced in the document set. Build one row per COC item.

| # | Item Certified in COC | Date OP Certified as Served | Document Found in Production | Match? | Concern |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | Yes / No / Partial | Yes / No / Partial | |

**After the table, note each of the following if present:**

- Items certified as served that are not present in the production
- Items in the production that were not listed in the COC
- Date discrepancies between the COC and the documents themselves
- Whether the COC was signed by the OP or by OP's counsel
- Whether a Certificate of Service is attached to the COC
- Whether the COC appears to have been filed vs. only served

### If no COC has been served by the OP:

State: "No Certificate of Compliance has been received from the opposing
party as of [date of this review]. If the mandatory disclosure deadline has
passed, this is a deficiency. See Section 5 — Potential Next Steps."

Note the OP's mandatory disclosure deadline if known from the disco tracker.

## SECTION 4 — FA ALIGNMENT REVIEW

This section is a preliminary cross-reference only — not the full Stage 3/6
FA review. The purpose is to flag obvious disconnects between the OP's FA
and the documents they produced. Stage 3/6 will conduct the full analysis.

**Run this section only if the OP FA has been received.**

If the OP FA has not been received, state that and flag it as a missing
mandatory disclosure item.

### Part A — Accounts and Assets Listed on FA vs. Documents Produced

| FA Item | Account / Asset Type | FA States | Document Produced? | If Produced — Account Confirmed? | Flag? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | Yes / No | Yes / No / Partial | |

Flag any of the following:
- Account listed on FA but no statement produced
- Statement produced for account not on FA
- FA lists real property but no deed or mortgage statement produced
- FA lists retirement account but no statement produced
- FA lists vehicle but no title produced
- FA lists business interest but no business documents produced

### Part B — Income Sources on FA vs. Documents Produced

| Income Source on FA | FA Claimed Amount | Supporting Document Produced? | Document Type | Preliminary Match? | Flag? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | Yes / No | | Yes / No / Unclear | |

Flag any of the following:
- Income claimed on FA with no supporting pay records, W-2, or tax return
- Employer listed on FA but no pay stubs from that employer produced
- Self-employment income claimed but no Schedule C, business return, or
  business bank records produced
- Rental income listed but no lease, mortgage statement, or Schedule E produced
- No documents at all to support any income category

### Part C — Preliminary FA Observations

List any preliminary items that stand out from this initial review that
should be examined in depth during Stage 3/6. Keep these factual and
specific. Do not draw legal conclusions.

Examples of appropriate flags:
- "FA claims monthly gross income of $X; no W-2 or pay stub produced to
  support this figure."
- "Three bank accounts identified in documents — only one listed on FA."
- "FA states no business interest; deposits in produced bank statements
  include apparent business-related transfers."
