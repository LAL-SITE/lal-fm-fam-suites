# Feinstein & Mendez, PA Financial Affidavit — Preliminary Review
## Stage 3 (and Stage 6 Second Party) — Financial Discovery Review Workflow

---

Rely only on the uploaded Financial Affidavit and information provided by the

values.

---

## What to Do

Read the Financial Affidavit carefully in its entirety before flagging anything.
Then produce the Preliminary FA Review Memo below.

This stage reviews the affidavit **on its own** — before any supporting
documents are compared. The goal is to identify what the party claims, catch
errors internal to the affidavit itself, and build the checklist of items to
verify in Stage 4.

---

## Step 1 — Identify the Form

Determine which form was used:

| Form | Version | When Permitted |
|---|---|---|
| 12.902(b) | Short Form | Only permitted when gross annual income is under $50,000 |
| 12.902(c) | Long Form | Required when gross annual income is $50,000 or more |

Flag if the short form (12.902(b)) was used but claimed income is at or near $50,000/year.
Note: per Rule 12.285(e)(1), a party under $50,000 may still be required to
complete the long form (12.902(c)) by interrogatory request or court order.
Flag if the form version is unclear or cannot be confirmed from the document.

---

## Step 2 — Extract and Summarize All Claimed Figures

Extract every figure stated on the affidavit. Organize into four categories:

**Income**
List every income source claimed: wages, salary, overtime, bonuses,
commissions, self-employment, rental income, interest, dividends, retirement
income, Social Security, disability, any other source. Note the amount and
whether it is stated as gross or net.

**Monthly Expenses**
List all expense categories claimed and the monthly amounts. Note any category
that appears blank, zero, or unusually low.

**Assets**
List every asset identified: real property, bank accounts, retirement accounts,
vehicles, business interests, personal property of value, other assets. Note
the value stated and whether the account or asset is described with enough
specificity to locate (institution name, last four digits, address, etc.).

**Liabilities**
List every liability identified: mortgages, car loans, credit cards, student
loans, personal loans, other debts. Note the balance stated and the creditor.

---

## Step 3 — Internal Review Checks

Run each check below. Flag every issue found.

**Form and Math Checks**
- Do the income subtotals add up correctly?
- Do the expense subtotals add up correctly?
- Does net income equal gross income minus stated deductions?
- Are asset totals and liability totals correctly summed?
- Is the correct form (long vs. short) being used for the stated income level?

**Income Checks**
- Is income stated as gross or net? (Affidavit requires gross income reported
  with deductions itemized separately)
- Are payroll deductions listed for a salaried or hourly employee? (Flag if absent)
- Is overtime listed? If so, is it described as routine or occasional?
- Is rental income stated gross or net? (Rule 12.902 requires net of ordinary
  and necessary expenses — flag if gross is used)
- Is self-employment income reported? If so, is a business expense schedule
  attached or referenced?
- Are any income sources listed without a corresponding employer or source name?

**Expense Checks**
- Are any major expense categories blank or zero that would be unusual for
  this household? (housing, utilities, food, transportation, insurance,
  child-related expenses if children are present)
- Do total monthly expenses appear inconsistent with stated income level —
  either far exceeding income with no explanation, or implausibly low?
- Are any expense items duplicated across categories?

**Asset Checks**
- Are accounts identified with enough specificity to subpoena if needed?
  (institution + last four digits or account type at minimum)
- Are any assets listed with no stated value or "unknown" value?
- Are real property addresses provided?
- Are retirement accounts identified by plan type and institution?
- Are business interests identified with entity name and ownership percentage?

**Liability Checks**
- Are all liabilities identified with creditor name and balance?
- Are any liabilities listed without a corresponding monthly payment in the
  expense section, or vice versa?

**Internal Consistency Checks**
- Does the stated income level appear consistent with the stated lifestyle,
  expenses, and asset base?
- Are there expense categories (private school, boat, club memberships, luxury
  items) suggesting a higher income than claimed?
- Does stated liquid asset level (checking, savings) appear low relative to
  income and monthly expenses?
- Does the affidavit appear to be signed and notarized? Flag if signature or
  notarization is absent or unclear.

## Preliminary FA Review Memo Format

```
INTERNAL CASE MEMORANDUM
TO:       Case File
FROM:     Financial Document Analysis Assistant
RE:       [Case Name] — [Party] Financial Affidavit Preliminary Review
DATE:     [Current Date]
STATUS:   Internal Work Product — Attorney Review Required
```

**Section 1 — Form Identification**
Which form was used, whether it was the correct form for the stated income
level, and whether signature and notarization are present.

**Section 2 — Income Summary**
All income sources claimed, amounts, and whether stated gross or net.
Total monthly gross income as stated on the affidavit.

**Section 3 — Expense Summary**
Total monthly expenses as stated. Note any blank or unusually low categories.

**Section 4 — Asset Summary**
All assets listed with stated values. Flag any without values or without
sufficient identifying information.

**Section 5 — Liability Summary**
All liabilities listed with stated balances. Flag any without creditor
identification.

**Section 6 — Internal Flags**
All issues identified in Step 3 above, described specifically. Each flag
should state: what the issue is, where on the affidavit it appears, and
why it warrants follow-up.

**Section 7 — Cross-Check Skeleton (Hand-Off to Stage 4)**
Build the starting row set for the Stage 4 FA Cross-Check chart: one row per
FA line item (every income source, account, asset, and liability), with the
"What the FA States" column pre-populated and all other columns empty. Stage 4
begins from this skeleton — no FA line item may drop out of the chart simply
because no supporting document arrives for it. An FA item with zero supporting
documents stays in the chart marked Missing. This is the working checklist
for the FA Cross-Check skill.
