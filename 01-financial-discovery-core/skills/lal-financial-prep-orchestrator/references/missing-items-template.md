# Missing-Items Template Language

This file contains reusable, brand-kit-aligned language for the most common missing-item asks. Drop these into the client request email at Stage 5. Adjust client name, dates, and account numbers per matter.

## VA Disability Award Letter (most common RED FLAG ask)

> **VA Disability Award Letter.** Current VA Disability Compensation Award Letter showing your monthly benefit amount.
>
> – Easiest source: VA.gov → sign in → "Get your VA benefit letters" → "Benefit Summary Letter"
> – Alternate: My HealtheVet portal, or call VA at 800-827-1000
> – If you can't access today, it's okay — but please tell us tomorrow what your current monthly disability amount is so we can move forward

## Monthly Household Expenses (the entire Section II)

> **Monthly household expenses.** Your best estimate of what you spend each month, in these categories. Don't overthink it — round numbers are fine for now and we'll refine on the call.
>
> – Mortgage payment (or send a recent statement and we'll pull it)
> – Utilities: electric, water/sewer, gas/propane, trash, internet/cable, phone
> – Groceries and household supplies
> – Vehicle: gas, insurance, registration, maintenance
> – Health-related: prescriptions, copays, dental/vision (anything Medicare/insurance doesn't cover)
> – Personal: clothing, hair/nails, dry cleaning, pet expenses
> – Entertainment, dining out, vacations (monthly average)
> – Anything else you pay regularly

## Accounts Found in Discovery But Not on Intake

> **Accounts to confirm.** We pulled your [N] months of bank statements and found [N] account(s) that weren't on your intake. Please confirm:
>
> – [Bank] [Account Type] ending [last 4] — [sole/joint], $[balance] as of [date]
> – Were these intentionally left off intake, or do you want them on the FA? Please also tell us if there are any other accounts (yours or joint) we don't yet have

## Vehicle Values

> **Vehicle values.** Best estimate of current value (KBB, Edmunds, or your own gut number) for each, plus year/make/model/VIN if handy:
>
> – [Vehicle 1]
> – [Vehicle 2]
> – [Vehicle 3]
> – [Vehicle 4]

## Minimum Monthly Payments on Cards & Loans

> **Minimum monthly payments on credit cards and loans.** For each card and loan listed below, the easiest path is to forward a recent statement so we can pull the minimum monthly payment:
>
> – [Creditor] ending [last 4]
> – [Creditor] ending [last 4]
> – ...

## Mortgage Payoff Reconciliation

> **Mortgage payoff reconciliation.** Two payoff figures — $[X] and $[Y] — appeared in your intake forms. Send a recent [Lender] mortgage statement and we'll lock in the right number.

## Trust Documents (Separate Property)

> **[Relation]'s trust documents.** The trust instrument and a recent statement of the trust corpus (and any distribution records, if you receive periodic distributions). The petition pleads the trust as your separate property; having the documents in hand locks that protection in.

## Life Insurance & SBP

> **Life insurance.** Any policy you carry on your life, plus any policy where you're a named beneficiary on someone else's life (especially [spouse]'s, if applicable, including any military SBP).

## Federal Retirement Source Documents (CSRS vs. FERS, TSP, DFAS)

> **[Spouse]'s federal retirement records.** When you can, please send what you have for each of these — even a screenshot from a portal works:
>
> – Most recent SF-50 (Notification of Personnel Action) — confirms FERS or CSRS
> – Most recent OPM annuity estimate or LES showing retirement deductions
> – Most recent TSP account statement
> – Most recent DFAS Retiree Account Statement (RAS) for military retired pay
>
> These are not on the FA itself but they're necessary for the post-decree retirement orders that come after the divorce is finalized.
